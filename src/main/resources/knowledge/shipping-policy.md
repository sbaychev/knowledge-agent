# Shipping Policy

Document version: 1.0

This is demonstration policy content for the Knowledge Agent project.
Replace it with the actual product policy before using the agent for real customers.

## Processing time

Orders are normally prepared for dispatch within two business days.

## Delivery estimate

The estimated delivery window is three to five business days after dispatch.
This is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date.

## Tracking

A tracking number becomes available after the carrier accepts the shipment.
For the current tracking number or shipment status, consult the live
shipment lookup tool. This policy document is not a source of live shipment data.
